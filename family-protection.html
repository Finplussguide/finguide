🛡️ Protect the plan, not just the goal

Building a future fund is only one part of financial planning. Families may also need protection against unexpected events.

❤️

Why family protection matters

If an earning family member dies unexpectedly, the family may face expenses and financial commitments at the same time that income is affected.

  • Household expenses
  • Outstanding loans or liabilities
  • Children's education
  • Future financial goals
  • Other family responsibilities
🛡️

Where life insurance fits

Life insurance is generally designed to provide financial protection to nominees or beneficiaries if the insured person dies, subject to the policy's terms and conditions.

  • Protection needs can differ from person to person.
  • Coverage should be reviewed against income and responsibilities.
  • Policy terms, exclusions and conditions matter.
  • Different products can have very different structures.
